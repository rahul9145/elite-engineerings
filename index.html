How we work

From raw material to your assembly line.

A disciplined, four-stage process that builds trust — durable, precise parts, delivered the way you need them.

01
Stage one

Material selection

Every requirement checked for hardness, strength, wear resistance and machinability before a single cut.

02
Stage two

Cutting & shaping

CNC, VMC and sliding-head machines precisely cut, shape and form the material to drawing.

03
Stage three

Finishing & QC

Finishing processes improve surface quality and hold tight tolerances, verified by measurement.

04
Stage four

Packaging & delivery

Parts are carefully packed for safe transport — straight to your stores or assembly line.

Quality assurance

The highest quality, by design.

Empowering your drive with top-quality, durable and reliable spare parts — backed by methods that catch defects before they reach you.

QA-01

Six Sigma & Lean

Lean manufacturing and Six Sigma methods minimise defects and keep every batch inside stringent quality standards.

QA-02

Non-destructive testing

Advanced NDT methods detect internal defects without damaging the part — so what you receive is what was specified.

QA-03

Full traceability

QR codes and RFID tags trace every part back to its manufacturing origin — accountability you can audit.
