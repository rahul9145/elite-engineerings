Company overview

Built in 2021. Trusted across industries.

Our manufacturing unit has rapidly emerged as a reliable and innovative provider of high-quality precision components.

With a commitment to excellence, we specialise in delivering superior spare parts crafted to meet the diverse needs of industries such as automotive, healthcare and engineering — using advanced CNC and sliding-head technology to produce components with exceptional accuracy and durability.

  • Precision-first machining — CNC, VMC, Traub and Citizen sliding head.
  • Proven at scale — 4,000 stretcher trolleys supplied through the pandemic.
  • Built around you — new component design or refinement of existing parts.
